Balance:
This refers to whether the image is equally weighted on the left and right, and to a lesser extent on the top and bottom. Basically, does this image feel like it's tilting to one side

Complexity:
How many different objects are in the image? How detailed are they? In other words, how busy is the painting?

Emotionality:
Does the painting portray or inspire strong emotions? This is subjective, obviously

Dynamic:
Does the painting portray dynamic gestures? Does it appear full of movement?

Naturalistic:
How much does the painting look like a photo?

Texture:
How rough or smooth is the painting's texture? Did the artist employ a lot of brustrokes?
